The automation backbone of We Digital Mitra — the systems that let a 1-human agency operate like a team:

- **Multi-agent swarms** for agency operations — research, outreach drafts, and content pipelines coordinated across specialized agents
- **Lead-gen pipelines** — Google Maps + spreadsheets + Claude for prospecting local businesses, run on a weekly cadence
- **WhatsApp automation** for client communication and follow-ups
- **CLI tools** in Python, Rust, and Node.js for the repetitive work in between

Every pipeline logs what it did, so a human can always verify the machine's work.
