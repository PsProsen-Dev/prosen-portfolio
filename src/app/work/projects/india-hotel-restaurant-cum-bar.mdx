Client project for **India Hotel & Restaurant Cum Bar** — a hotel, restaurant and bar on NH-27 (old NH-31) at Tekatali, Maynaguri, run by its owner-operator.

Scope delivered:

- Business website at [indiahotelmaynaguri.com](https://indiahotelmaynaguri.com) — the full ~170-item food and bar menu, room tariffs, gallery, and click-to-call / WhatsApp actions
- Google Business Profile setup and optimization
- Social media presence setup
- Business email on the client's own domain

The site went live in September 2026 with analytics and Search Console wired from launch week — so the owner can see exactly what the website is doing for the business.
